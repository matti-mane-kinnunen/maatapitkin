User guide

This map shows how far you get from __ORIGIN__ by train and bus – to the 50 US state capitals, Washington, DC, and Canada's capitals – Ottawa and the provincial capitals – door to door. It is meant to show that it can be done and roughly how long it takes, not to replace a journey planner: check the times and book with Amtrak, VIA Rail and the bus companies.

Pick a destination

  • The list below the map shows the capitals in order of arrival, with the door-to-door time. The colour tells on which day you arrive.
  • Click a city in the list or on the map, or type its name in Find a city (press /).
  • The journey's timetable appears beside the map (below it on narrow screens), day by day: every train and bus, the changes, and where you stay overnight. On the map, numbered marks show where each day begins and a bed shows a hotel night.
  • Some capitals have no journey yet: Juneau and Honolulu (no train or bus); Frankfort, Carson City, Helena, Bismarck and Pierre, which neither Amtrak nor Greyhound / FlixBus serves (other bus companies do, but their timetables are not in the data yet); and in Canada Victoria, Fredericton, Charlottetown, St. John's and the three territorial capitals, which VIA Rail does not reach. Regina is reached by bus.

Choose how you travel

  • Leave: the travel day. Some long-distance trains run only three days a week (the Cardinal, the Sunset Limited), so the day changes the answer.
  • From: leave in the morning or later in the day, when the long-distance trains leave.
  • Other date or time …: choose any date the imported timetables cover, any start time, a longer or shorter change time, or no buses, and press Show journeys. Your browser works the journeys out with the same rules and timetables as the map (it takes a moment). Back to the map's journeys returns to the usual view; Copy link keeps your settings.
  • Night trains: allowed uses trains and buses that run through the night – most of Amtrak's long-distance trains do, with seats and sleeping cars; avoid travels by day only, so long journeys take many hotel nights.
  • Arrive by, above the list, shows only the places you reach by that day; No hotel nights only journeys without one; Include other cities adds the hubs and the ends of the lines (San Diego, Miami, Churchill, Prince Rupert …).

Read a journey

  • Where there is a real choice, cards show Fastest (arrives first), Fewest changes and Fewest hotel nights, each with its departure and arrival.
  • A wait through the night is a hotel night: you sleep in that town and go on in the morning. Nights on a train or a night bus are spent on board.
  • Clock times are local; the door-to-door time accounts for time zones (Chicago to Seattle crosses two).
  • A change takes at least an hour between trains, and 45 minutes to or from a bus: Greyhound and FlixBus stops can be a few kilometres from the station.
  • The CO₂ line compares the journey with flying; How? explains the estimate. Amtrak's trains outside the electrified Northeast Corridor run on diesel.
  • Copy link gives a link to exactly this journey and these choices – to share or save.
  • Under each stretch of train travel, Book … on amtrak.com opens Amtrak's site with the station codes and the departure to type in – Amtrak has no link that fills them in. VIA Rail's trains and the buses have no link; book them on viarail.ca and with Greyhound or FlixBus.
  • Add to calendar downloads the journey as a calendar file (.ics) – every train, bus and hotel night at its local time. It is made in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Report a problem beside a train opens a new GitHub issue with that train already filled in (you need a GitHub account) – e.g. when it does not run that day.
  • When the destination has a map of its own (New York, Seattle, New Orleans, Miami), Continue from … at the end opens it – to plan the next part of a longer trip.

The map

  • Zoom with + and − (or the mouse wheel, or pinch) and drag to move; Fit shows all of it.
  • The thin red lines are Amtrak's whole train network and the small dots its stations; their names appear as you zoom in. The journeys are drawn along the tracks; a faint dashed line is a bus whose road is not in the data.
  • How far in, above the map: move the slider to see which capitals you reach within that many hours, door to door; the others fade.
  • Point at a connection to see its departures.
  • The map draws the connections that journeys use; All connections shows the rest of the data.
  • Maps from at the top opens the same map for other starting cities; Other maps the European maps.

Where the times come from

The timetables are Amtrak's, VIA Rail's and FlixBus / Greyhound's own open data (GTFS), downloaded and imported every week (VIA Rail's: contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada); other bus companies are not included yet. Journeys are worked out for the travel days shown, with time for every change – the settings are in the text just above. The CO₂ figures for trains are the US EPA's (VIA Rail's diesel trains get the same figure), for buses and flights the same Swedish figures as on the European maps. Map outlines: Natural Earth. The page counts visits with GoatCounter: no cookies and no personal data, and not at all if your browser asks not to be tracked.
