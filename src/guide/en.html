User guide

This map shows how far you get from __ORIGIN__ by train, ferry and bus – to the capitals in Europe and around the Mediterranean, door to door. It is meant to show that it can be done and roughly how long it takes, not to replace a journey planner: check the times and book with the operators.

Pick a destination

  • The list below the map shows the capitals in order of arrival, with the door-to-door time. The colour tells on which day you arrive.
  • Click a city in the list or on the map, or type its name in Find a city (press /).
  • The journey's timetable appears beside the map (below it on narrow screens), day by day: every train, ferry and bus, the changes, and where you stay overnight. On the map, numbered marks show where each day begins and a bed shows a hotel night.

Choose how you travel

  • Leave: the travel day. Some trains and ferries do not run every day, so the day changes the answer.
  • From: leave in the morning or in the evening.
  • Night trains: allowed uses sleepers and night ferries; avoid travels by day only (night ferries still count). Some places are reachable only by night train.
  • Arrive by, above the list, shows only the places you reach by that day; No hotel nights only journeys without one; Include other cities adds towns and ports that are not capitals.

Read a journey

  • Where there is a real choice, cards show Fastest (arrives first), Fewest changes and Fewest hotel nights, each with its departure and arrival.
  • A wait through the night is a hotel night: you sleep in that town and go on in the morning. Nights on a sleeper or a night ferry are spent on board.
  • Clock times are local; the door-to-door time accounts for time zones.
  • The CO₂ line compares the journey with flying; How? explains the estimate.
  • Copy link gives a link to exactly this journey and these choices – to share or save.
  • Under each stretch of train travel, Check … on bahn.de opens Deutsche Bahn's journey planner at that departure – to check the trains and to book. It may suggest other trains; night trains and Eurostar may show no price there. Ferries and buses have no link.

The map

  • Zoom with + and − (or the mouse wheel, or pinch) and drag to move; Fit shows all of it.
  • Point at a connection to see its departures.
  • The map draws the connections that journeys use; All connections shows the rest of the data.
  • Maps from at the top opens the same map for other starting cities.

Where the times come from

Most trains are looked up every week in Transitous, an open community journey planner; ferries, buses and trains it lacks are entered by hand from Seat61, Deutsche Bahn and the operators. Journeys are worked out for the travel days shown, with time for every change – the settings are in the text just above. DB station numbers for the bahn.de links come from Trainline's open station list (ODbL).
